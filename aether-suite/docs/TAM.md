# Total Addressable Market (TAM)

## Inputs

- Target segments:
  - SMB and mid-market revenue teams
  - Compliance-led B2B vendors
  - Agencies and consultants producing proposals and landing pages
- Average annual contract value estimate: **$4,800**
- Initial serviceable market assumption: **150,000 organizations** globally

## Top-down estimate

`150,000 orgs × $4,800 ACV = $720,000,000`

Estimated TAM: **$720M annual software spend**.

## Bottom-up validation lens

- If 8,000 customers are acquired across all products at $4,800 ACV:
  - Annual revenue potential: **$38.4M**.
- Expansion revenue via shared billing and cross-sell can materially increase LTV.
